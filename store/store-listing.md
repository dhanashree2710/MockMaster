**App name (max 30):** MockMaster: AI Interview Coach
**Short description (max 80):**

**Full description (max 4000):**
MockMaster helps you walk into interviews prepared.

Upload your resume, paste the job description of the role you want, and MockMaster builds a practice plan around the gap between the two.

WHAT YOU CAN DO
• Resume analysis – see the skills, experience and projects found in your resume
• Resume ↔ job match – get a match score, strengths and the skills you are missing
• Preparation plan – a focused to-do list for the role you are targeting
• AI mock interviews – questions tailored to your resume and the job, from easy to hard, in a friendly, professional or strict style
• Speak or type your answers – voice answers use your device's speech recognition
• Instant feedback & reports – clarity, relevance, depth and delivery scores with improvement tips
• Progress tracking – see how your scores improve over time
• Practice in 10 Indian languages for the interviewer voice (English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Bengali, Gujarati, Kannada, Punjabi, Urdu)

PRIVACY FIRST
Your resume file is read on your device; only the extracted text is saved to your account. You can delete your account and all data at any time from Settings.

IMPORTANT
AI-generated questions and feedback can be imperfect. MockMaster is practice material, not professional or hiring advice. You can report problem content from the interview report screen.
