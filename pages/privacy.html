Privacy Policy

MockMaster — AI Interview Coach

MockMaster helps you practise job interviews using your resume and a job description. This policy explains what data we collect, why, who processes it, how long we keep it and how you can delete it. By creating an account and ticking the consent box you agree to it.

In short
  • We collect only what the app needs: your account details, resume text/profile, job descriptions, interview answers and results.
  • We do not sell your data, show ads, or use advertising IDs, location, contacts or photos.
  • We do not record or store your voice — only the text of your answer.
  • Resume text and job descriptions are sent to an AI provider (Groq) to generate questions and feedback.
  • You can delete your account and all your data at any time, in the app or on the web.

2. How we use your data

  • To create and secure your account and sign you in.
  • To analyse your resume, compare it with a job description and build a preparation plan.
  • To generate interview questions and feedback, and to produce reports and progress charts.
  • To prevent abuse, enforce usage limits, and review reports of unsafe AI content.
  • To respond to your support and deletion requests.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling for third parties, or selling to data brokers. Our legal basis is your consent (given at sign-up) and the need to provide the service you asked for.

3. Microphone and voice

If you choose to speak your answers, the app asks for microphone permission. Speech is converted to text by the speech-recognition service built into your device or browser (on Android this is provided by Google and may process audio on Google's servers under Google's privacy policy). MockMaster does not record, upload or store your audio; only the resulting text answer is saved. You can type your answers instead and deny or revoke the permission at any time in your phone's settings. The interviewer's spoken questions use your device's built-in text-to-speech.

4. AI processing

Interview questions and feedback are generated by a third-party large-language-model provider (currently Groq). When you use an AI feature, we send — through our secure server function and only while you are signed in — the relevant content: a summary of your resume (name, skills, roles, projects), the job description, and your answers when feedback is requested. We do not send your email address, phone number or password. The provider returns text to us and acts as a service provider for this purpose; we do not authorise it to use your content to build advertising profiles.

Optional own AI key: in Settings you may enter your own API key for Groq, Google Gemini or OpenAI. If you do, requests from your device go directly to that provider under its own terms and privacy policy, and the key is stored only on your device.

AI output can be wrong, biased or inappropriate. It is practice material, not professional, legal or hiring advice, and no hiring decisions are made about you. You can report a problem from the interview report screen.

6. Data stored on your device

To work smoothly (and partly offline) the app keeps some data in your browser/app storage and cache: your sign-in session, a copy of your current resume profile, job, match, recent sessions and progress, in-app credits, your theme and language choices, and cached app files. We do not use advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. This device data is removed when you sign out or delete your account; you can also clear it by clearing the app's storage in your phone settings.

7. Storage, security and retention

  • Data is stored in Supabase with Row Level Security so each user can only read and change their own records, and is transmitted over HTTPS (encrypted in transit).
  • Our AI provider key is kept only on the server and never shipped in the app.
  • We keep your data until you delete your account. Inactive accounts may be removed after a long period of inactivity after we try to contact you.
  • When you delete your account, your data is erased from our live database immediately. Backups held by our database provider may retain copies for a limited period (typically up to 30 days) before being overwritten.
  • Aggregated AI-request counters are removed with your account.

No system is perfectly secure; we cannot guarantee absolute security, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and regulators where the law requires if a breach affects you.

8. Your choices and rights — including deletion

  • Delete your account and all data instantly: in the app go to Settings → Delete my account & data, or use this web page. If you cannot sign in, email us (below) from your registered address and we will delete it within 30 days.
  • Access and correct your information from your profile, or ask us.
  • Withdraw consent at any time by deleting your account; this does not affect past processing.
  • Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 you may request access, correction, erasure and grievance redressal, and nominate someone to exercise your rights. Where laws such as the GDPR or CCPA apply to you, you have similar rights, including to complain to your local data protection authority.

9. International transfers

Our service providers (Supabase, Groq) may process data on servers outside your country, including outside India. By using the app you understand your data may be transferred there, subject to appropriate safeguards in our agreements with those providers.

10. Children

MockMaster is intended for adults seeking jobs (18+). We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has an account, contact us and we will delete it.
